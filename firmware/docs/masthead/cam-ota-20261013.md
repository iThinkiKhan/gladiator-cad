# CAM OTA bench — October 13, 2026

Final firmware: **masthead-1.1.2**, installed and confirmed over recovery Wi-Fi. See [setup and recovery](../MASTHEAD.md#camera-ota-and-recovery-before-sealing-the-case) and [aggregate record](cam-ota-summary-20261013.json).

- Missing/wrong keys returned 401; wrong application marker returned 400 before flashing.
- A deliberately corrupted full image transferred completely, then ESP-IDF rejected its appended digest (`ESP_ERR_OTA_VALIDATE_FAILED`).
- A connection closed after a partial upload retained the current boot and left maintenance available.
- A complete Wi-Fi upload booted 1.1.1 in app1 with pending verification. With confirmation withheld, the board returned to 1.1.0 app0 after its 120-second deadline.
- Two subsequent Wi-Fi updates were confirmed, switching app0 → app1 → app0. The final 1.1.2 maintenance-page build was then installed and confirmed in app1. Both slots contain OTA-capable firmware.
- Public builds contain no LocalConfig.h or maintenance key. The board retained its provisioned network and key through every wireless update. Recovery WPA2/API authentication remained usable without the C6.
- Arduino's default reconnect loop initially disrupted recovery AP traffic. Disabling it, disconnecting the failed station attempt, and suspending station scans while a recovery client is connected resolved complete transfers.
- The Windows recovery profile initially required manual reconnection after reboot. Rejoining recovery Wi-Fi before the confirmation deadline is necessary if the client does not reconnect automatically.
