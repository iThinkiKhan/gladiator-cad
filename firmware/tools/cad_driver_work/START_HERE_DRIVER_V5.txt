GLADIATOR DRIVER MOUNTS v5 -- MORNING PRINT ORDER

1. Open Gladiator_DriverV5_INTERLOCK-FIT-COUPON.3mf in OrcaSlicer.
   Use EnderNeo, PLA, 0.20 mm layers. Keep its saved orientation.
   Supports OFF; add a 5 mm brim for the tall wedge section.
   Print and test the full-length tongue/groove and locator fit first.
   Clear brim/elephant foot before judging the fit. If it binds or is loose,
   stop before printing the full mounts and record the result.

2. If the coupon fits, open Gladiator_PlateI_DRIVER-V5-INTERLOCK.3mf.
   It contains both bases and both wedges, already arranged for a 220 x 220 bed.
   Do not auto-orient or rearrange them.
   Use EnderNeo + PLA, 0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 30% infill, 5 mm brim.
   Enable normal automatic supports from the build plate only. Preview the
   supports under the wedges before starting. Slice in OrcaSlicer in the morning.
