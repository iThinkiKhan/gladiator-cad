# Speaker rail bracket v1 (shell off) - DRAFT, 2026-10-20 (Claude)

**Draft. Not printed.** Built by `build_speaker_rail_bracket.py`. With the shell on, the speaker sits in the hood's roof cradle (see the enclosure README, v2.7); with the shell off it goes on this bracket.
* Bolts to the LEFT rail's two rear hood inserts (rail outer face; CAD (Y22, Z40) and (Y35.5, Z46)) with 2 x M3 x 10. The right rail keeps the switch and INA leads.
* 3 mm plate on the rail face, two 7 mm posts with M2 pilots (36.5 apart) holding the speaker by its ears, cone facing OUTWARD over the left track; the speaker stands 5.2 mm off the plate so the M3 heads fit behind it.
  Order: screw the plate to the rail first, then the speaker onto the posts (M2 through the ears from the front).
* Speaker (Jim): 28 x 31 x 15, ears 44 tip to tip, holes 36.5. Ears 2.8 thick, centred between the front and rear faces, M2 (Jim 2026-10-21).
* Checked against the rails as installed, deck v4 (also moved +-2 mm), battery holder and cells, tray, tracks: no collision. The speaker is 2.0 mm under the deck edge and 0.67 mm outboard of the battery holder.
* Print: rail face down, posts up, no supports. Plate SPK: 21 minutes, 4 g (posts 11.3 mm to the ear plane).
