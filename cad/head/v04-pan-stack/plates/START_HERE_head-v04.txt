GLADIATOR HEAD v0.4 - PRINT PLATE 1 (2026-10-01)

FILE:  Gladiator_Head_v04_PLATE1_Pedestal-Retainer-Cap.3mf
       Three parts, about 27 g of PLA before supports. Do NOT auto-orient.

THESE THREE WAIT ON NO COUPON RESULT. The other four head parts (neck, rotor, servo carriage,
drive pulley) are NOT here on purpose: they need the final coupon plate results first.
Nothing here has been printed or fitted. It is a design candidate that passed the computer checks.

WHAT EACH PART IS
  Pan_Raised_Pedestal  The big round part with a 40-tooth ring in the middle and a flat
                       rectangular seat on top. The head's tilt unit bolts to the seat, and the
                       belt drives the toothed ring. 16.6 cm3, the biggest part.
  Pan_Retainer         A flat ring. It clamps the top bearing down.
  Neck_Clamp_Cap       The front half of the collar that clamps the head to the mast.

PRINT SETTINGS
  PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 30-40% infill.
  SUPPORTS ARE NEEDED FOR THE PEDESTAL ONLY: under the big round top plate and the flat seat
  (turn on normal / auto supports). Look at the slicer preview before you print:
    - there should be NO support between the two flanges of the toothed ring (the belt groove)
    - the teeth must not be touched by support
  The retainer and the cap need none. A 3 mm brim is fine for the cap, which is narrow.
  Elephant-foot compensation on if your profile has it.

WHAT TO LOOK AT AFTERWARD
  - Teeth: crisp, not mushy (the 2-notch profile that fitted your belt).
  - The four 2.6 mm screw holes in the pedestal's base plate and in the retainer.
  - The two hexagonal pockets on the underside of the pedestal's rectangular seat take an M3 nut.

If you would rather wait for the coupon results first, nothing is lost by waiting.
