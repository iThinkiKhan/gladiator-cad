WHAT EACH PIECE IS (the first nine are done; only these six are left):

H3b-1, H3b-2, H3b-3   bearing posts (a flat disc, a post, a groove near the top)
     Sizes 19.95 / 20.05 / 20.15. The notches on the disc rim tell them apart (1, 2, 3).
     Do:  start with the 1-notch post. Slide a 6804 bearing on BY HAND down to the disc.
          If it will not go on by hand, STOP, pull it off by hand, try the next one up.
          On the best one, snap a circlip into the groove.
     Report: which posts the bearing slid onto, which is best, does it rattle, did the
          circlip seat.
     Never force it. The first post jammed a bearing so hard two pliers could not free it.

H4b   a small bar with three blind holes, 2.3 / 2.4 / 2.5 (1, 2, 3 notches on the side)
     Do:  try an M2 screw in each. Which holds firmly without splitting the plastic?

H6a, H6b   horn pockets (a flat plate with a cross-shaped pocket and a hole in the middle)
     H6a is a snug fit (1 notch), H6b an easy fit (2 notches).
     Do:  drop the cross horn in BOSS DOWN (the raised round hub goes into the round recess,
          flat side of the arms up). Does it sit flat and not rock or turn?
          Then take the screw that normally holds the horn onto the servo. Does the shank
          pass the 3.4 mm centre hole, and does the head sit ON the plate instead of
          falling through?
     Report: which of the two fits, how far the arms stand above the rim, and the screw.
