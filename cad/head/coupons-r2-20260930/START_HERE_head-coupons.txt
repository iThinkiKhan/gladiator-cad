GLADIATOR HEAD COUPONS, ROUND 2 - WHAT EACH PIECE IS FOR
(plain version; the full notes are in Gladiator_HeadR2_README.md)

None of these is a head part. Each one is a small test that answers ONE question
before the real head is printed. After each test, just tell Claude what happened.
Never force anything. If a part will not go together by hand, stop and report it.

H1a  60-tooth pulley   (the BIG toothed wheel, 41 mm across)
H1b  40-tooth pulley   (the SMALL toothed wheel, 28 mm across)
H1c  slotted bar       (long flat bar with one round hole and one slot)
     Question: how far apart should the two pulleys sit so the real belt is snug?
     Do:  M3 bolt up through the round hole, nut on top, put the 60T wheel on it.
          Second M3 bolt up through the slot, nut on top (finger tight), 40T wheel on it.
          Loop the belt round both. Slide the slot bolt out until the belt is snug,
          tighten it. Turn the wheels by hand: does the belt run clean?
     Report: calipers across the two bolts, outside to outside and inside to inside.
          And whether it ran clean.

H2a  neck collar, TIGHT key   (1 notch)   half-ring with two ears and a small tab inside
H2b  neck collar, LOOSE key   (2 notches) same, with a slightly smaller tab
H2c  clamp cap                (the other half-ring, no tab)
     Question: does the collar that will carry the head fit the top of the real mast?
     Do:  Try H2b first. Hook the half with the tab onto the top of the mast from the
          BACK of the robot, tab into the flat on the mast, push down till it sits flat.
          Fit H2c from the front, two M3 bolts through the ears, nuts, tighten.
          Then try H2a the same way.
     Report: does each slide on by hand, sit flat on the mast top, hold when tight,
          or twist when loose?

H3   bearing post   (flat disc at the bottom, a post, a groove near the top)
     Question: does a 6804 bearing slide on, and does a circlip hold it?
     DONE - the post was too tight. Claude is making a new set of posts at smaller sizes.
     Please do not push a bearing onto the old post again.

H4   small bar with three blind holes (notches on the side: 1, 2, 3 = smallest to largest)
     Question: which hole does an M2 screw bite into and hold, without splitting?
     Do:  Try an M2 screw in each, then one of the SG90 ear screws.
     Report: best hole for each screw.

H4b  same bar again with three BIGGER holes (1, 2, 3 notches = 2.3 / 2.4 / 2.5)
     NEW - the biggest H4 hole worked best, so this is the next size up. Same test.
     Not yet printed.

H5   bar with three hex pockets (1, 2, 3 notches = smallest to largest)
     DONE - the middle one (2 notches) takes an M3 nut perfectly. Thank you.

H6a / H6b   horn pockets (NOT printed yet - these two were added later)
     Question: does the real cross horn drop into the pocket? Horn goes in BOSS DOWN.

If you can't tell which piece is which, the file names say it, and the picture
preview-print-orientation.png shows all of them.
