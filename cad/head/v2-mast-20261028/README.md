From Jim's masthead v1 notes (2026-10-28). CAD and checks only: **nothing printed, sliced or released.**
Robot frame: mast is FRONT (+Y). The head is modelled in the v05 construction pose (sensor face -Y, legacy pan 0).

Source: `scripts/build_head_v2_mast.py`, GENERATED by `make_v2.py` (kept here) from `scripts/build_head_v05.py`

Whole mast + head assembly (tube, neck, pan stack, head, camera case), from `mass_mast.py`:

|---|---|---|---|
| v1 as built | 258.2 | 173.2 | 25.3 |

For the whole robot of mass M kg, v2 raises its centre of mass by about 7.8/M mm and moves it 3.1/M mm rearward (2 kg robot: +3.9 mm up, 1.6 mm back). Ground is world Z -36; the wheels touch down near Y -5 and Y 145; the mast is at Y 113. The robot has not been weighed, so tip angles are not computed. - Firmware tilt limits are unchanged; widen them only after a physical check.
